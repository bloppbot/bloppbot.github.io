Format

The Ability Draft World Cup returns. 8 teams, double-elimination, played across two days.

Day one opens with a seeding round of best-of-ones to place teams into the upper and lower brackets. Day two runs through the lower bracket matches, lower-bracket final, and grand final — all best-of-three.
