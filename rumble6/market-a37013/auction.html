HOW THE AUCTION WORKS

Captains: the 8 highest-rated players (AD rating + real Dota rank, real rank counts 1.5x). Everyone else goes up for auction, strongest first.

Log in (test mode): enter a Steam ID from the player list (the Dota friend ID or the 17-digit one) and you act as that player. Captains get bid buttons, everyone else watches. Real Steam login comes later.

Gold: an average captain gets . Stronger captains start with less, weaker ones with more.

Bidding: opening bid is at least 5, then +5 / +10 / +25 or type an amount. A fresh player waits 25s for an opener, every bid resets the clock to 12s (the admin can change both). Highest bid at 0 wins.

No all-in on one player: every open slot needs at least 5 gold, so your max bid = gold left minus 5 for each other open slot. Full teams can't bid.

No bids: the player goes to the back and comes around again once everyone has been up. Passed twice = unsold. If players run out before teams are full, the admin hands out the rest for 5 each, richest team first.

Suggested price = what the player would cost in the fixed-price market (same ratings). Only a guide, bid what you think he's worth.
