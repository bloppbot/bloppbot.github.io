The 8 highest values are captains. The 32 most expensive market players cost gold, so an average team of 4 costs . Base budget = that +10% = .

Handicap mode (default): each captain gets the base ± a quarter of the gap between his own price and the average captain price (). The best captain starts with the strongest team, so he gets the least gold. Equal mode: everyone gets the base. The mode can only be switched before the first pick.

Pick order: snake, weakest captain first. Round 1 goes from the cheapest captain to the most expensive, round 2 goes back. A buy is blocked if it leaves too little gold to fill the remaining slots. If nothing is affordable, the captain must take the cheapest player left.
