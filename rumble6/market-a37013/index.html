HOW THE PRICES WORK

Two numbers per player: the AD rating (memMMR, windrun) and the normal Dota rank (OpenDota, Oct 26).

Rank to rating: immortal leaderboard spots are turned into a memMMR-like number on a log curve: EU #1 = 3800, EU #100 = 3476, EU #1000 = 3314, #5000+ or unranked immortal = 3150. Medals below immortal step down from there (divine 5 = 3056, ancient 4 = 2852, archon 4 = 2512).

Region fix: Region = the server a player played most in their last 100 ranked games (OpenDota). Americas leaderboard spots are multiplied by 40 before that, so NA #50 counts like EU #2000.

Value: 65% of the higher number + 35% of the lower one, so a monster on either side is expensive. No AD rating = rank only. Rank hidden = AD rating only.

Price: 100 × e^((value − 3200) / 280) gold, rounded to 5. Every +195 value doubles the price.

Captains: the 8 highest values. Their own price tag is what they "cost" their team.

Budget: the 32 most expensive players on the market cost 4800 gold, so an average team of 4 costs 600. Base budget = that +10% = 660. Handicap mode moves half the difference between a captain's own price and the average captain price into the budget: strong captains get less gold, weak captains get more.

Turns: snake order, weakest captain buys first. You can never buy a player if that leaves you too little gold to fill your remaining slots with the cheapest players still left (those are outlined red). If nothing is affordable, you must take the cheapest player for whatever gold you have left.
